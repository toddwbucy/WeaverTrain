# STE100 rewriter: DeepSeek V4 Pro and Gemini 4 Argon on a 100-segment kit (2026-11-01)

Follows `2026-11-01-ste-rewrite-round3.md`. The tooling for this run is on branch
`ste/500-run` (commit `1a9f603`), not yet pushed.

## Why 100 segments, and why DeepSeek

The first 500-segment run (450 narration, 50 final reports) was void:

- DeepSeek V4 Pro passed 208/500, but it changed its drafts with regex substitution
  scripts (`fix.py`, `fix2.py`, `fix3.py`). Its own report called them drafts.
- Gemini 4 Argon passed 69/500. 230 of its 500 outputs were over 95% identical to
  the input. It wrote its records through a generator script that it then deleted.

The operator then ruled a smaller test: 100 segments with one model, and a longer run
only if this one is good. Gemini was the planned model. Its provider returned HTTP 401
on every request from 17:49Z, so DeepSeek V4 Pro ran the test first (18:08Z to 18:46Z).
Gemini's provider accepted requests again later, and Gemini ran the same kit, same
prompt and same rounds in a fresh session (19:44Z to 20:03Z).

## What changed since the void run

- **The prompt forbids changing text with a program** in plain terms: no
  find-and-replace, regex, `sed` or `str.replace`. The model must keep every script it
  writes. A near-copy of the input is not a rewrite.
- **`score` checks for cheating.** A model directory that holds a script with
  `re.sub`, `.replace(`, `.translate(` or `sed s/` marks the round INVALID. Outputs over
  95% similar to their input are counted as near-copies.
- **The kit reads in batches.** The model reads 10 records at a time and re-reads
  `RULES.md` every 100 records.

## The kit

100 segments drawn from the void run's 500 (seed 2030): 90 narration and 10 final
reports, one per trajectory. The bins are 37 under 200 masked characters, 30 from 200
to 500, and 33 of 500 or more. The prompt is the round 3 prompt plus the prohibitions
(`e21370fcd140`).

## Meaning review

Two Claude reviewers per model read every input against its final rewrite, 50 each.
The scale is the round 2 scale:

- faithful: same facts and certainty;
- minor: drift a reader would not act on;
- wrong: a reader would understand something different;
- data: a concrete value changed.

| Bin (masked chars) | n | DeepSeek F / m / W / D | Gemini F / m / W / D |
|---|---|---|---|
| under 200 | 37 | 37 / 0 / 0 / 0 | 35 / 2 / 0 / 0 |
| 200 to 500 | 30 | 24 / 6 / 0 / 0 | 26 / 4 / 0 / 0 |
| 500 and over | 33 | 20 / 11 / 1 / 1 | 18 / 14 / 0 / 1 |
| **all** | 100 | **81** / 17 / 1 / 1 | **79** / 20 / 0 / 1 |

By kind: of the 10 final reports, DeepSeek has 7 faithful and Gemini 6. Neither model
has a wrong final report.

The two models are a tie on meaning: two segments apart on faithful, and DeepSeek has
the one wrong verdict. The round 2 review used 40 long segments per model and found
DeepSeek 15 faithful, 4 wrong, 2 data and Gemini 17 faithful, 2 wrong, 6 data. Here
the long bin is 20 and 18 of 33 faithful, with at most one wrong and one data each. The
kits differ, so this is a direction, not a measured gain.

### The serious cases

- **DeepSeek t066, wrong.** "they're supposed to operate independently" became "they
  operate independently". An expectation became a fact, and this contradicts the bug
  that the passage goes on to describe.
- **t074, data, both models.** A sentence quoted from the issue was reworded by both.
  Quoted issue text inside narration is not masked; masking it closes this case.
- **Gemini t002, minor but close to wrong.** "broadcast with [[C13]] [[C28]]" became
  "with [[C13]] and [[C28]]": a qualifier became a second operand. Two adjacent
  placeholders invite this.

### Patterns in the minor drift (both models)

- **Certainty moves.** An option the agent was still weighing becomes a decision
  (t036, t060 in both). A guess becomes a claim (DeepSeek t031, Gemini t070 "should be"
  -> "must be"). "likely" weakens to "possibly" (DeepSeek t092).
- **Domain terms flatten.** "feature" became "option" (t100, both), "whole slide
  images" became "whole/full images" (t048, both), "tracked" became "recorded"
  (DeepSeek t030), "concurrent" became "parallel" (Gemini t068).
- **Wrong sense of an approved word.** "extract" became "remove" (DeepSeek t039,
  t048); "work around" became "prevent" (Gemini t071).
- **The same segments drift for both models** (t001, t036, t039, t048, t053, t060,
  t074, t100): these are where the restricted vocabulary has no exact word for the
  source term, so a ruling or a technical-name entry would help both.
